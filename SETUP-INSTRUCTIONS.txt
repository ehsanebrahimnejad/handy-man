IMPORTANT: WEBSITE REVIEWS
The website is already programmed to save reviews locally and attempt to save them online.
For reviews to appear to every visitor and remain after refresh on every device, complete this one-time Google setup:

1. Create or open a Google Sheet.
2. Copy the long Sheet ID from the address between /d/ and /edit.
3. Open script.google.com and create a new project.
4. Paste the contents of GoogleAppsScript.gs into Code.gs.
5. Replace PUT_YOUR_GOOGLE_SHEET_ID_HERE with your Sheet ID.
6. Click Deploy > New deployment > Web app.
7. Execute as: Me.
8. Who has access: Anyone.
9. Deploy and copy the new Web App URL.
10. Open index.html and replace the SCRIPT_URL near the bottom with the new URL.
11. Upload the updated index.html to GitHub.

Your current Google Apps Script URL is already in index.html. If you update that existing deployment with the included GoogleAppsScript.gs code, you may keep the same URL by editing the deployment and creating a new version.
